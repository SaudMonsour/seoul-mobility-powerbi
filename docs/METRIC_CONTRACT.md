# Metric contract

Total Rentals sums observed rentals. Operating Hours sums the binary operating flag; Recorded Hours counts all source hours including 295 closures. Rentals per Operating Hour divides rentals by operating hours under current filters. All closed hours have zero rentals in this snapshot. Operating Coverage divides operating by recorded hours. Peak Single Hour is the maximum observed rental count, not a capacity measure. Average Temperature C uses all selected recorded hours, including closed hours.

## Filter context

Dimension slicers propagate through active one-to-many, single-direction relationships to the fact table. KPI and chart measures are evaluated in the selected context. A chart selection can further narrow other visuals, subject to native Desktop interaction defaults. Blank ratios mean no valid denominator; they must not be interpreted as an observed zero. Tooltips expose record counts and, for campaign conversion, descriptive interval bounds.

## Snapshot caveats

This is one historical year for one city. No fleet size, station capacity, inventory or unserved requests are provided. Counts do not establish utilization or unmet need. Weather patterns and holiday differences are descriptive, not controlled effects.
